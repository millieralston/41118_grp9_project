Project Overview

TipBot is a Group 9 robotics project by Melroop, Millie, and Eliza. It simulates two Husky UGVs in PyBullet: a runner that follows scripted evasion behavior and a chaser that learns to intercept using reinforcement learning.

Key components

  • PyBullet simulated environment with obstacles and arena boundary.
  • Runner robot with scripted evasion policy.
  • Chaser robot trained with PPO and future CNN visual input.
  • Reward design including catch reward, distance shaping, obstacle penalties, and stuck penalties.

Goals

Demonstrate reinforcement learning, reward engineering, and perception in a robotics pursuit task.
