TipBot

Hello everyone, we are Group 9. My name is Melroop with my teammates Millie and Eliza. Our AI in robotics project is called the TipBot.

Project summary

Our system is designed to simulate a reinforcement learning system, where two Husky bots are loaded within a PyBullet simulated environment to chase another Husky bot while simultaneously avoiding obstacles.

The TipBot system trains a robotic UGV (unmanned ground vehicle) to follow and intercept ground targets. Through this project, we hope to better demonstrate our understanding of the topics learned throughout this course.

Current progress

  • Developed a fully built environment where two Husky robots are initialized on the ground at random locations.
  • Implemented random obstacle placement with trees, benches, and a boundary fence.
  • The runner follows a scripted policy to evade the chaser robot.
  • The chaser is the training subject and pursues the runner.
  • Implemented reward functions with both dense and sparse signals.

Reward function

  • +200 for catching the runner
  • −0.8 × distance per step to encourage closing the gap
  • −5.0 obstacle/wall proximity penalty
  • −5.0 stuck penalty for low movement

Challenges

  • Version conflicts between group members caused inconsistencies when running PyBullet, especially related to camera image data.
  • Code was updated to handle variable observation formats robustly.
  • Training with CNN image inputs requires GPU support instead of CPU.
  • Observation space design needed enough useful information without overwhelming the model.
  • Initial trial showed the chaser robot not moving, pointing to the need for better reward and action scaling.

Next steps

  • Improve reward policy for active pursuit and obstacle avoidance.
  • Enhance runner scripted movement to create a more meaningful training challenge.
  • Improve collision avoidance reliability for both Husky robots.
  • Fully implement the CNN-based policy using forward-facing camera input.
  • Add multi-frame stepping and parallel training to improve speed.

Technical inputs

  • Observation: 64 × 64 × 3 RGB camera image, 60° FOV, near/far clip 0.1 / 100.0.
  • Action: 2 continuous controls [linear_velocity, angular_velocity].
  • Scaled in env: linear = action[0] × 4.0, angular = action[1] × 2.0.
  • PyBullet stepSimulation() per action step; demo runs at 240 Hz.
  • Training runs in DIRECT mode for uncapped speed.
  • Episode setup: 12 random trees, 5 random benches, and random spawn positions inside ±10.

PPO training configuration

  • Vectorized environments: n_envs = 4
  • Policy: CnnPolicy
  • n_steps = 2048
  • batch_size = 128
  • gamma = 0.99
  • gae_lambda = 0.95
  • clip_range = 0.2
  • ent_coef = 0.01
  • vf_coef = 0.5
  • max_grad_norm = 0.5
  • Total timesteps = 200,000

Demo notes

We validated the environment tracks the runner and nearby obstacles during movement. Visual markers show the runner and obstacles updating correctly. The model still needs better reward tuning to move reliably and avoid tilting from high velocity.
